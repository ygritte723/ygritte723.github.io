**Coauthored journal article · Published 17 August 2026**

This study integrates 227 features from 984 older adults across clinical risk factors, physical function, body composition, biomarkers, metabolomics, and cardiac imaging. It evaluates feature selection and interpretable machine-learning models for an impaired myocardial relaxation phenotype.

The primary XGBoost benchmark achieved ROC–AUC **0.8157 in cross-validation** and **0.7658 on the test set**. The distinction matters: performance on new data was lower than the internal cross-validation estimate, and independent validation remains necessary.
