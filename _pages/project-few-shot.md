<h2>The problem</h2><p>Gastrointestinal images can exhibit subtle visual differences, while labeled examples are limited and endoscopic viewpoints vary.</p>
<h2>Our approach</h2><p>The model combines a lightweight Conv4 feature extractor with task interpolation, self- and cross-relational embeddings, and bi-level routing attention. Support and query examples are compared through relational representations, while task interpolation diversifies the training tasks.</p>
<h2>Recognition</h2><p>This first-author paper received an Honorable Mention Award at IEEE CAI 2024 and was presented orally.</p>
