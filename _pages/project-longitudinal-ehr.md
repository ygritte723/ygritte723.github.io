<p class="eyebrow">Ongoing project · Transformers & multi-agent collaboration</p><p class="intro">Understanding how maternal health trajectories relate to infant outcomes.</p><h2>Data & task</h2><p>The project links maternal and infant EHR records, laboratory results, and vital signs across time. I use a Transformer model for longitudinal risk stratification, investigating potential transgenerational health associations.</p><h2>Collaboration design</h2><p>I am designing specialized agents for EHR history, laboratory trends, and vital signs to bring evidence from different time points together. Agent roles and evidence synthesis remain under development.</p><p>The work studies risk and associations; it does not establish causal transgenerational effects or claim clinical deployment.</p>
